Customer's Review

Hands down the best bakery in town! I ordered a custom birthday cake and a dozen croissants for a weekend brunch. The cake was not only gorgeous but incredibly moist, and the croissants had that perfect, buttery, flaking crust you usually only find in Paris. The staff was super friendly when I picked everything up, and the shop smelled like absolute heaven. This is officially my go-to spot for every special occasion!

Aizal

Decent local bakery with a lovely atmosphere. The morning buns and coffee were great, but the cinnamon roll I tried was a bit on the dry side and lacked enough filling. It’s a bit pricier than other spots nearby, so I was hoping for total consistency across the board. I'll probably come back to try their savory items, but some of the sweet pastries could use a little tweaking.

Aliza

The baked goods here are top-tier. Their sourdough loaf has an incredible crust and chew, and the chocolate chip cookies are completely addictive—crispy edges with a soft, gooey center. The only reason I'm giving 4 stars instead of 5 is that the line moved pretty slowly on Saturday morning, and they had run out of a few popular pastries by 10 AM. Get there early if you want the full selection, because the food itself is worth every penny.

Ali Akbar
